Il Significato

A Name Born from Nature

The word frasca in Italian means a branch or bough — a sprig of greenery, something living and rooted. It is one of those surnames that tells you exactly where it came from: the land itself.

In medieval Italy, a frasca was also the sign hung outside a rural tavern. A branch above the door meant wine was being served inside — warmth and company awaited. The Frasca family may well have been the keepers of that door, a family known for hospitality and gathering.

Whether the name came from the forest or the tavern, the meaning is the same: life, growth, and welcome.

La Storia

The Tavern Branch

"In medieval Italy, a branch hung above a doorway meant one thing: come in, sit down, you are welcome here. The frasca was the sign of the tavern — and perhaps the sign of a family whose name became synonymous with warmth, gathering, and place."

Long before printed signs and painted boards, Italian tavern keepers hung a simple green branch above their door to signal that wine and company were inside. The branch — the frasca — became so associated with hospitality that it gave birth to an expression still heard in Italy today: "andare alla frasca" — to go to the tavern.

Whether your ancestors grew the branches, lived among them, or poured wine beneath one, the Frasca name carries something worth holding onto: a deep connection to the Italian land, and a tradition of welcome.

La Cronologia

A History Across Centuries

1300s

First Recorded in Florence

The Frasca surname appears in 14th-century Florentine tax records — among the earliest documentation of the name in Italian history.

1500s

Rise in the Republic of Venice

The Frasca family gained prominence in Venice, with members serving as merchants and diplomats representing the Republic's interests across European courts.

1890

Coat of Arms Recorded

The Frasca coat of arms — D'azzurro alla croce piena d'oro — is formally documented in the Dizionario storico blasonico delle famiglie nobili italiane.

Late 1800s

The Great Migration from Montefalcone

Like thousands of families from the mountains of Campania, the Frascas of Montefalcone di Val Fortore joined the great wave of Italian emigration — leaving behind the hills northeast of Naples and carrying their name to the United States and beyond.

Today

A Name in 39 Countries

The Frasca name now spans 39 countries. Rare enough to be remarkable — about 1 in every 576,000 people on Earth carries it. The village of Montefalcone di Val Fortore still stands in the hills, 817 metres above the sea.
